# Cleanora – Complete Page Architecture

Cleanora includes 43 fully coded HTML pages categorized into 3 distinct modules.

---

## 1. Public Website Pages (19 Pages)

1. `index.html` – Home Page 1 (General Services Landing with 4-step process and trust highlights)
2. `index-2.html` – Home Page 2 (Room-by-Room Cleaning focus with Before-and-After interactive slider)
3. `about.html` – About Us, company history, 5-step cleaner verification and quality checklist
4. `services.html` – Services catalog with category filtering (Deep clean, routine, room-specific, relocation)
5. `service-details.html` – Deep Home Cleaning detailed 64-point checklist & preparation guidelines
6. `pricing.html` – Transparent flat-rate packages, recurring subscription discounts, add-on pricing
7. `booking.html` – Multi-step interactive booking engine with real-time price calculations in INR
8. `booking-confirmation.html` – Booking confirmed state with reference #STK-84920 and invoice print
9. `team.html` – Certified cleaning staff profiles with verification badges and customer ratings
10. `blog.html` – Cleaning advice, checklists, and seasonal maintenance guides
11. `blog-details.html` – Full article view with author details, table of contents, and share links
12. `contact.html` – Contact form, Google Maps placeholder, business hours, emergency inquiry
13. `faq.html` – Categorized frequently asked questions with smooth accordion animations
14. `login.html` – Customer portal login with prefilled demo credentials
15. `register.html` – Customer registration with terms and privacy acceptance
16. `forgot-password.html` – Password recovery interface with success feedback state
17. `404.html` – Custom 404 error page with navigation rescue links
18. `coming-soon.html` – Expansion launch announcement with animated countdown timer
19. `maintenance.html` – Scheduled server maintenance notice with emergency helpline

---

## 2. Customer Dashboard Portal (11 Pages)

Located in `customer-dashboard/`:
1. `index.html` – Overview: Upcoming appointment card, KPI statistics, quick booking CTA
2. `bookings.html` – My Bookings: Filter tabs (All, Upcoming, Completed, Cancelled), reschedule/cancel modals
3. `booking-details.html` – Detailed booking view: cleaner assigned, room checklist, payment summary
4. `recurring-plans.html` – Active subscriptions: weekly/biweekly/monthly plans, pause/resume toggle
5. `payment-history.html` – Completed transactions, UPI/card mode labels, payment statuses
6. `invoices.html` – Sales Tax Tax invoice printable document with itemized tax breakdown
7. `addresses.html` – Saved service addresses book: default address setter, add/edit modal
8. `messages.html` – Customer notifications: cleaner arrival updates, discount vouchers
9. `reviews.html` – Ratings and reviews submitted by customer
10. `profile.html` – Customer profile details, avatar upload demo, change password
11. `settings.html` – Notification preferences (WhatsApp, SMS, Email) and account preferences

---

## 3. Admin Operations Dashboard (13 Pages)

Located in `admin/`:
1. `index.html` – Operations console: monthly revenue in INR, total cleanings, dispatch matrix, revenue chart
2. `customers.html` – Customer management: search, tier filters, lifetime spend, profile links
3. `bookings.html` – All Bookings: status filters (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled)
4. `services.html` – Service catalog management: price configuration, duration, Add Service modal
5. `categories.html` – Category configuration with service counts
6. `cleaners.html` – 42 active cleaning professionals: availability status, ratings, dispatch
7. `recurring-plans.html` – Recurring subscription renewals, active subscribers
8. `payments.html` – Live payment gateway capture logs (UPI, Card, Net Banking)
9. `invoices.html` – Business Sales Tax invoice register with print/download actions
10. `messages.html` – Customer support inquiries inbox with quick reply modal
11. `reviews.html` – Review moderation feed: approve/hide customer reviews
12. `reports.html` – Quarterly revenue and service popularity analytics
13. `settings.html` – Legal entity details, EIN, working hours, pricing rules
