Full Home Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

An intensive 360-degree cleaning designed to eradicate accumulated grime, kitchen grease, bathroom hard-water scale, and hidden allergens across your entire residence.

What's Included: Room-by-Room Checklist

Our 3 to 4 member team adheres strictly to this 64-point checklist verified by an on-site supervisor before sign-off:

1 Kitchen Intensive Degreasing

  • Range Hood exterior, baffles, and grease traps cleaned with industrial degreasers.
  • Gas stove knobs, burner stands, and surrounding tile backsplash descaled.
  • Kitchen sink, chrome faucets, and drains cleared of hard-water calcium deposits.
  • Cabinets and drawers wiped clean externally and internally (if empty).

2 Bathroom Acid-Free Descaling & Sterilization

  • Acid-free chemical scrubbing of all wall tiles, floor grout, and shower corners.
  • Hard water salt stain removal from glass shower partitions, mirrors, and taps.
  • Complete sanitization of toilet pot, jet spray, washbasin, and vanity.

3 Living Room, Bedrooms & Floor Buffing

  • Ceiling fans, AC filters, exhaust fans, and electrical switchboards dusted and wiped.
  • Wardrobes, TV units, desks, and door frames wiped free of dust and fingerprints.
  • Mechanized single-disc floor scrubber applied to marble, vitrified tile, or hardwood.
  • Dry vacuuming of sofa, cushions, curtains, and mattresses.
