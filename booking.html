FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Common Booking Questions

Do I need to be present during the entire service?

No. Many clients let the crew in and return for the final quality inspection. All our cleaning crew members are Aadhaar-verified and fully insured.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel my booking?

You can reschedule or cancel at zero penalty up to 2 hours before your scheduled arrival window through your customer dashboard or via phone.

Do I need to supply any vacuum cleaners or chemicals?

Never. Our team arrives in uniform with industrial Karcher wet/dry vacuums, steam sanitizers, microfiber sets, and eco-certified Diversey solutions.

How are prices calculated for large homes?

Our flat-rate pricing covers standard bedrooms and common areas up to standard square footage. For custom estates, our estimator applies flat tiered rates without surprise charges.
